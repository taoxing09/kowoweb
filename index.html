Positioning

Not an app category. A community finance layer.

KOWO is designed as a platform that can power many use cases across Africa and its diaspora without becoming the regulated holder of customer funds.

Why KOWO

Built to outgrow “digital savings”.

Most platforms focus on one product. KOWO focuses on the infrastructure needed by communities, institutions and payment partners.

Community first

Designed for groups, governance, transparency, contribution cycles and long-term trust.

Partner first

Works with regulated institutions for funds, wallets and compliance responsibilities.

Compliance by design

Clear separation of software, payment, regulated account holding and platform fees.

Enterprise readiness

A public partner stack.

KOWO is already packaged for serious discussions with microfinances, banks, PSPs and institutional partners.
