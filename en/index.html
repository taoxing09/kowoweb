Architecture

Each player stays in its role.

KOWO provides the experience and orchestration. Licensed partners hold accounts, wallets and financial operations according to the applicable framework.

1 · UserJoins a community
2 · KOWOOrganizes rules, tracking and records
3 · InstitutionOpens account or wallet
4 · PSPExecutes payment and payout
5 · CommunityTracks and decides with trust
Core principle: user funds should remain with a licensed financial institution or payment partner. KOWO only receives its platform fee under the selected contractual model.
